Text to Speech

Bring your words to life with Canva's Text to Speech tool. Easily turn written text into natural-sounding speech. Choose from a variety of AI voices to enhance your videos, ads, presentations, podcasts, and more—no mic or recording needed.

Effortlessly give your projects a voice

Make your content more engaging and memorable with voiceovers. Use text to speech on Canva and instantly add emotion, tone, and personality that text or visuals alone can't convey. Simply provide your script and watch our tool generate a voiceover for your content that will keep your audience hooked.

Bring your characters to life

Give your characters a voice with natural-sounding voiceovers, perfect for virtual influencers, VTubers, and anyone who uses digital avatars⁠(opens in a new tab or window). Make sure to include your preferred tone and voice, such as masculine, feminine, conversational, professional, or playful, in your text prompt. Make your characters' lines even more dynamic by opening the editor and using Magic Write⁠(opens in a new tab or window), powered by OpenAI, to craft compelling dialogue, then bring it to life with AI text-to-speech.

Create instant audiobooks you can enjoy anywhere

Turn any written text into an audiobook in seconds. Perfect for auditory learners or people with visual impairments, the text-to-speech generator on Canva makes reading easier and more convenient. Now, you don’t need to sit down and read—enjoy your favorite story anytime, anywhere.

How to convert your text to voice

Frequently asked questions

  • Text-to-speech technology turns writing into spoken words, allowing your computer, smartphone, or other devices to "read" text aloud to you. This makes digital content more accessible, especially for people with visual impairments or reading difficulties.
  • The text-to-speech feature can convert a maximum of 2,000 characters. If your text is longer, no worries! Simply break it into smaller sections and convert each one separately. You can then merge the audio files and create a seamless listening experience using our free video editor. Alternatively, if you need to convert audio recordings to text, you can use our audio to text converter.
  • Text-to-speech uses advanced algorithms and natural language processing to create voices that sound human, capturing nuances like pronunciation, tone, pitch, speed, and rhythm.
  • You can translate any text into a voiceover in a wide range of languages, such as:
    • English
    • Spanish
    • Mandarin Chinese
    • Arabic
    • Filipino
    • German
    • Greek
    • French
    • Japanese
    • Italian
    • Korean
    • And many others

    With multi-language support, you can create voiceovers and narrations that connect with all kinds of people, no matter where they’re from. Whether you're reaching out to your local community or aiming for an international audience, you'll easily find a language option that fits your needs.
