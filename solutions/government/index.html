Frequently Asked Questions

  • Canva empowers everyone in your government office—from public affairs to parks and recreation to HR—to create high-impact visual content with confidence and ease. Government teams produce professional, on-brand materials without the bottlenecks and lengthy approval cycles that often slow down public sector work. With one secure, centrally managed platform, government agencies can work faster, stay consistent, and reduce reliance on external design resources.

  • Canva Enterprise is our recommended plan for government teams, offering the advanced security, administrative controls, and compliance features public sector organizations require. While smaller teams may explore Canva Business, only Canva Enterprise delivers the brand governance, data protection, support, and scalability needed to meet strict government standards. Contact our sales team⁠(opens in a new tab or window) to learn how Canva Enterprise can support your agency’s needs.

  • Canva is used by a wide range of government departments, including Communications, Public Affairs, Community Services, Digital Strategy, Parks and Recreation, Emergency Management, Police and Fire, Public Health, Transportation, and Human Resources and Recruitment. It’s designed to support inter-agency collaboration and help every department communicate with clarity and consistency.

  • Reach out to our sales team⁠(opens in a new tab or window) for a tailored solution to fit your needs.

  • Canva Enterprise is our recommended plan for government, offering the advanced security, administrative controls, and compliance features public sector teams need. Pricing is based on your team’s size and requirements, and we offer discounted rates for eligible government organizations. For a custom quote, contact sales⁠(opens in a new tab or window).

  • Canva Enterprise includes dedicated support and services to help your organization succeed. All users receive 24/7 support, while Admins get case manager assistance. Organizations with 100 or more contracted seats also gain access to a dedicated Customer Success Manager, creative services, and support for IT onboarding and technical setup. Learn more about our professional services here⁠(opens in a new tab or window).

  • Canva Enterprise offers flexible billing options to meet your agency’s procurement processes, including the ability to set up invoicing on a regular schedule. To learn more or request invoicing, reach out⁠(opens in a new tab or window) to our sales team.

  • Canva Enterprise is designed with the security, compliance, and administrative control needs of government agencies in mind. It includes robust identity and access management features such as Single Sign-On (SSO) and SCIM provisioning, allowing IT teams to manage users efficiently and securely. Advanced admin capabilities—like role-based access controls, audit logs, content approvals, and AI feature management—support compliant collaboration across departments. Canva meets globally recognized security standards, including SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR, and continues to invest in trust, transparency, and data protection.

  • Canva is committed to making design accessible for everyone. We have achieved WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across our core product experiences and offer built-in accessibility tools including accessible PDF exports with semantic tagging and reading order, alt text and decorative images, Accessibility Checker (Docs & Presentations), auto-captions for videos, and zoom support up to 400%. We follow global accessibility standards, conduct regular audits, and publish Accessibility Conformance Reports (ACRs) aligned with the Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT). Learn more about Canva’s accessibility commitment and access our latest ACRs here⁠(opens in a new tab or window).

  • You can contact our sales team⁠(opens in a new tab or window) to discuss your specific needs.
