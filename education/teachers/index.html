Canva for Teachers

Spark creativity in your classroom with Canva Education – your all-in-one tool to create, collaborate, and inspire. It’s 100% free for verified teachers and students.

Get verified - Canva Education(opens in a new tab or window)Learn more(opens in a new tab or window)

Why teachers love Canva Education

Enjoy powerful design tools, seamless collaboration, endless templates, and time-saving AI – free for teachers and students.

Teach and learn with an all-in-one tool

  • Design made simple: Create lesson plans, presentations, sheets, posters, and more with easy drag-and-drop tools.
  • Built-in student access: Invite students to collaborate seamlessly using their school email – no extra software or logins needed.
  • Save time with templates: Customize pre-made designs for every teaching need in just a few clicks.

Engage your students, ignite creativity

  • Assign tasks and track progress: Assign tasks, monitor progress, and let students shine at their own pace – all from one easy-to-use dashboard.
  • Create engaging experiences: Build interactive games and activities that transform passive students into active learners.
  • Add interactivity in class: Run quick polls and quizzes to check understanding, bring data to life with visual charts, and annotate live with Draw.

Save time and boost productivity with AI

  • Accelerate lesson planning: Effortlessly generate lesson plans, teaching ideas, and student activities in seconds with Magic Write.
  • Make learning accessible: Easily convert formats, translate, and add video captions. Use our accessibility checker to help make content more inclusive.
  • Give your teaching materials a glow up: Upload any file and use Canva’s AI-powered editing tools to give it a refresh.

Get verified with these simple steps

Here’s how to get started with Canva Education.

Step 1

Tell us which country and school you teach at.

Step 2

You might be eligible for instant verification. If not, you’ll need to upload a document to confirm your teaching status. You should hear from us within 24 hours, however in busy periods it may take up to 7 days.

Step 3

You’re in! Enjoy full access to Canva Education – 100% free. Invite your students and fellow teachers to start collaborating.

  • Canva Education is available for K-12 (primary and secondary) educators, their students, and qualified school districts and institutions. Check out our full eligibility guidelines.⁠(opens in a new tab or window)

  • Teachers can sign up by visiting the sign up page⁠(opens in a new tab or window), creating an account, and completing the verification process.

    If you already have a Canva account, you can upgrade it to Canva Education.

  • Yes. If you’re a teacher with existing Canva account, simply log in, visit the sign up page⁠(opens in a new tab or window) and follow the verification steps. All previous designs and projects will remain in your account.

  • All premium Canva Pro features are included in Canva Education, as well as dedicated features for educators:

    • Premium design tools (normally in Canva Pro)
    • Education-specific templates for lesson planning and activities
    • LMS integrations (Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology, etc.)
    • Collaboration tools for teachers and students
    • Safe-for-school content filtering

  • Canva Education provides thousands of free templates for lesson plans, worksheets, classroom posters, and presentations. Teachers also get access to premium fonts, images, and graphics at no additional cost.

    Once you’ve signed up, make sure to browse our Education Library⁠(opens in a new tab or window) or search by grade or subject to find the resource on any topic you need.

  • Our dedicated education and district offerings are FERPA and COPPA compliant, and we are also a signatory of the National Data Privacy Agreement. We are committed to GDPR compliance, and offer safe-for-school content.

  • Students must be invited by their teacher, school or school district.

    You can invite your students from your Canva Education by entering their email addresses, sharing invite links, or importing your Google Classroom class. Learn more here⁠(opens in a new tab or window).

Along with a global group, we have an array of local teachers communities for several languages and locales. Learn more⁠(opens in a new tab or window).
